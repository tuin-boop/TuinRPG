TuinRPG v0.8.2 - Episode selection restored

Choosing TuinRPG now offers the loaded game's episodes when more than one exists,
restoring episode selection for Ultimate Doom and other episode-based campaigns.
Escape returns to the mode menu. Ultra-Violence remains the fixed engine skill.

Starting a campaign from the menu no longer grants console-warp catch-up rewards.
Doom II still starts directly at MAP01. Episode monster scaling now starts from
the selected episode's first map instead of inheriting E1's map-level offset.
New normal campaigns default to Normal RPG difficulty; Hell's Gambit stays Hard.

Validated with UZDoom 5.0.3: Freedoom Phase 1 episode selection, return navigation,
E2M1 starting at player level 1 without catch-up weapons, and Doom II MAP01 on
Ultra-Violence. Ultimate Doom itself was not available for this regression test.

Download TuinRPG.pk3 and load only one TuinRPG package. Restart the engine after
updating. Back up saves before updating. Older releases remain available.
