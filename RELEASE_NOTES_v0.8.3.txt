TuinRPG v0.8.3 - Ammo-pressure waves and Doom 1 loot fix

The adaptive director checks full ammunition at 25%, 50% and 75% map kills only.
Each distinct full ammo pool used by an owned weapon triggers one wave of about
10 monsters, capped at five waves per checkpoint. Shared ammunition counts once.
Clear a wave before the next starts. Reinforcement kills do not advance checkpoints.

Waves use nearby positions along your recent route, with collision, visibility
and player-distance checks. Tight spaces can reduce or skip spawns. A larger,
tilted, animated INCOMING WAVE warning announces each wave.

Super Shotguns are excluded from Doom 1 RPG loot even when weapon mods supply
their graphics. Doom II keeps its Super Shotgun loot support.

Load only TuinRPG.pk3. Restart the engine after updating and back up your saves.
Previous releases remain available. Tested with UZDoom 5.0.3.
